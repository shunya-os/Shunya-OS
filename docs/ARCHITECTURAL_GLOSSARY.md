# SHUNYA Architectural Glossary

Version: 1.0.0

Status: Active

Depends On:

- SHUNYA Canon
- All Canonical Specifications

---

# Purpose

The Architectural Glossary defines the canonical meaning of architectural terms used throughout SHUNYA.

Every engineer.

Every designer.

Every architect.

Every artificial intelligence agent.

Every contributor.

Must use these definitions consistently.

The glossary prevents ambiguity.

Architecture begins with shared language.

---

# Design Principles

Every definition shall be:

Canonical.

Business-centric.

Technology-independent.

Future-proof.

Provider-independent.

Universally applicable.

---

# Object

A business entity with identity.

An object represents something meaningful within an organization.

Examples:

Customer.

Project.

Invoice.

Meeting.

Document.

Task.

Objects possess identity,

relationships,

history,

memory,

knowledge,

and lifecycle.

---

# Relationship

A meaningful connection between two or more objects.

Relationships provide business meaning.

Relationships are first-class architectural entities.

---

# Event

An immutable record describing something that occurred.

Events never change.

Events generate organizational history.

---

# Timeline

The chronological reconstruction of events.

Timelines explain how organizational reality evolved.

---

# Memory

Validated historical understanding retained by the organization.

Memory preserves context.

Memory supports reasoning.

---

# Knowledge

Institutionally validated understanding.

Knowledge represents organizational truth.

Knowledge evolves through validation.

---

# Knowledge Graph

The connected representation of organizational understanding.

Objects.

Relationships.

Knowledge.

Memory.

Events.

All connected.

---

# Search

Contextual discovery of organizational understanding.

Search returns meaning.

Not merely records.

---

# Context

The complete information required for intelligent reasoning.

Context is assembled.

Never manually maintained.

---

# Workspace

A contextual environment where organizational work occurs.

Workspaces project business understanding.

---

# Workflow

A governed progression of business state.

Workflows coordinate execution.

---

# Automation

Repeatable execution governed by rules.

Automation performs predictable work.

Humans retain judgment.

---

# Agent

A persistent artificial intelligence execution entity.

Agents pursue goals.

Not conversations.

---

# Execution

The controlled progression of work toward measurable outcomes.

Execution updates organizational understanding.

---

# Communication

Business interaction connected to organizational context.

Communication becomes organizational intelligence.

---

# Document

A first-class organizational knowledge object.

Documents participate in relationships,

memory,

knowledge,

and intelligence.

---

# Financial Object

A measurable financial entity participating within universal architecture.

Examples:

Invoice.

Payment.

Expense.

Budget.

---

# Notification

A contextual request for human attention.

Notifications direct work.

Not interruption.

---

# Analytics

Measured interpretation of organizational activity.

Analytics explain organizational behavior.

---

# Identity

A human,

system,

automation,

or artificial intelligence participant within the platform.

---

# Permission

An explicit authorization to perform an action.

Permissions remain explainable.

---

# Policy

A governing rule controlling platform behavior.

Policies override implementation.

---

# Integration

A controlled connection between SHUNYA and external systems.

Integrations exchange business meaning.

---

# Runtime

The execution environment responsible for platform operation.

Runtime remains invisible to business users.

---

# Deployment

The controlled promotion of software into operational environments.

Deployment never changes business architecture.

---

# Tenant

An independently managed organization within SHUNYA.

Tenants remain architecturally isolated.

---

# Plugin

An extension built upon stable platform contracts.

Plugins never modify the platform core.

---

# SDK

The development toolkit enabling creation of native platform extensions.

---

# Observability

The capability to understand platform behavior through evidence.

Observability explains systems.

---

# Bootstrap

The deterministic initialization of a new SHUNYA installation.

Bootstrap prepares the platform for operation.

---

# Evolution

The governed progression of SHUNYA over time.

Evolution preserves architectural integrity.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

A reasoning capability operating within organizational governance.

Artificial Intelligence assists.

Humans remain accountable.

---

# Universal API

The stable architectural contract connecting every interface to SHUNYA.

Every capability communicates through the Universal API.

---

# Canon

The authoritative architectural source.

The Canon defines how SHUNYA shall be designed,

implemented,

and evolved.

Conflicts are resolved in favor of the Canon.

---

# Specification

A normative architectural document.

Specifications define required platform behavior.

Specifications are authoritative.

---

# Engine

A platform capability responsible for one architectural concern.

Examples:

Workflow Engine.

Memory Engine.

Search Engine.

Execution Engine.

Each engine owns one responsibility.

---

# Organization

The highest business boundary inside SHUNYA.

Organizations own:

People.

Knowledge.

Memory.

Objects.

Relationships.

Execution.

Policies.

---

# Business Truth

The validated organizational reality represented by SHUNYA.

Business truth evolves through events,

memory,

knowledge,

and governance.

Business truth is never inferred without evidence.

---

# Architectural Integrity

The preservation of architectural principles across every platform evolution.

Integrity always overrides convenience.
