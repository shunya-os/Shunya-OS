# Purpose

The Financial Engine manages the financial reality of an organization.

Financial information is not isolated accounting data.

It is connected to customers, projects, approvals, documents, relationships, workflows and organizational intelligence.

The Financial Engine preserves financial accuracy while enriching business understanding.
