# Purpose

Intelligence is the defining capability of SHUNYA.

Data informs.

Knowledge explains.

Intelligence reasons.

The purpose of intelligence is not replacing people.

Its purpose is enabling better human decisions.

---

# Definition

Within SHUNYA,

Intelligence is the ability to understand organizational context,

reason using accumulated knowledge,

predict likely outcomes,

recommend better actions,

and continuously improve organizational capability.

---

# Intelligence Architecture

Intelligence operates through six stages.

Observe

↓

Understand

↓

Reason

↓

Recommend

↓

Learn

↓

Improve

Every intelligent capability follows this cycle.

---

# Observation Layer

Observation collects facts.

Facts include:

Events.

Object changes.

Relationships.

Documents.

Meetings.

Messages.

Approvals.

Searches.

User actions.

Automation outcomes.

Observation never interprets.

Observation records reality.

---

# Understanding Layer

Understanding connects observations.

The objective is answering:

What happened?

Why?

How does it relate to existing knowledge?

Understanding builds context.

Context precedes reasoning.

---

# Reasoning Layer

Reasoning evaluates context.

Reasoning should identify:

Patterns.

Dependencies.

Contradictions.

Risks.

Opportunities.

Missing information.

Historical similarities.

Reasoning never assumes certainty.

Reasoning produces evidence.

---

# Recommendation Layer

Recommendations emerge from reasoning.

Recommendations should include:

Suggested Action.

Supporting Evidence.

Expected Outcome.

Confidence.

Alternative Approaches.

Potential Risks.

The recommendation remains optional.

---

# Learning Layer

Learning occurs after outcomes become known.

The operating system compares:

Expected Outcome.

↓

Actual Outcome.

↓

Difference.

↓

Updated Understanding.

Learning improves future reasoning.

Historical facts remain unchanged.

---

# Improvement Layer

Improvement applies accumulated learning across the organization.

Knowledge compounds.

Reasoning improves.

Recommendations become increasingly contextual.

The organization becomes progressively more capable.

---

# Intelligence Principles
