## Product Completeness (Article V)

**If a founder installs SHUNYA tomorrow, can they operate a complete business?**

**Yes — for the core business functions:**
- CRM (customers, leads, contacts, deals) ✅
- Invoicing + payments ✅
- Task/project management ✅
- Email + WhatsApp communication ✅
- Document management ✅
- AI assistance for all outcomes ✅

**For specialized functions, they would need:**
- Accounting (payroll, tax) → partial (expense tracking exists, payroll pending)
- Marketing automation (SEO, brand management) → partial (campaigns exist, SEO pending)
- Industry-specific (manufacturing, restaurant, healthcare) → defined in ontology, implementation pending

**Dependency documentation:** SHUNYA eliminates 12+ SaaS tools (CRM, invoicing, project management, email marketing, document management, knowledge base, HR recruitment, travel booking, calendar, task management, proposal generation, AI assistant). Remaining dependencies are accounting-specific (payroll, tax) and industry-specific verticals.
