## Status Vocabulary

Only these statuses are permitted:
- **NOT STARTED** — No work has begun
- **IN PROGRESS** — Active work underway
- **BLOCKED** — Cannot proceed without dependency
- **PASS** — Exit criterion met with evidence
- **FAIL** — Exit criterion not met

Do not use: "mostly done", "essentially complete", "green enough", "ready pending X", "practically final", "99.5% complete".

## Dependency Rules

- No milestone may be marked PASS until its dependencies are PASS.
- No skipping ahead because a later area is convenient.
- A milestone can only be closed by Hermes with evidence and verified exit criteria — not by prose alone.
