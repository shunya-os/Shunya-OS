|-----------|-------|

| **Rows** | 11 |

### Legacy stores

| Store | Table | Rows | Status |
|-------|-------|------|--------|
| PersonIdentity (app/models.py) | `persons` | ~? | LEGACY |
| Identity engine (shunya/identity/) | (quarantined) | 0 | QUARANTINED |

### Resolution

- `PersonIdentity/Person` in app/models.py is legacy CRM identity — consumers should migrate to shunya_identities
- Identity engine in shunya/identity/ is quarantined — no live production calls
- Adapter boundary: `_resolve_identity()` in authz/decorators.py bridges session identity to OrgMember.identity_id

|-----------|-------|

| **Canonical persistence** | `EvidenceRecord` SQLAlchemy (app/evidence/models_db.py — `evidence_records`) |
| **Rows** | 68 |
| **Status** | **DUAL** — dataclass concept and SQLAlchemy persistence coexist |

### Resolution
- The `Evidence` dataclass in `app/evidence/models.py` is the canonical domain model
- The `EvidenceRecord` SQLAlchemy model in `app/evidence/models_db.py` is the canonical persistence
- These are separate layers — the dataclass is NOT persisted to SQL
- `InMemoryEvidenceStore` should be migrated to use `EvidenceRecord` for production persistence
