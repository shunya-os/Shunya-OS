# PRODUCT-00 — Productization Constitution

**Status:** PERMANENT
**Adopted:** 2026-08-06

---

## Principle

The architecture exists to serve users, not itself.

Every future improvement shall increase real-world usefulness while preserving
constitutional integrity.

## Architecture Freeze

No new Runtime, UCP, Living Object, or Internal Primitive may be introduced
unless the required behavior cannot be expressed through composition of the
existing architecture. Convenience and novelty are never sufficient.

Performance Review. No production change is complete without all five.
