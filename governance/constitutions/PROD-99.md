# PROD-99 — Production Constitution & Launch Governance

**Status:** PERMANENT — supersedes all temporary production guidance
**Adopted:** 2026-08-06

---

## Purpose

SHUNYA has transitioned from Architecture Creation to Continuous Product
Evolution. The purpose of SHUNYA is to become the world's most trusted
Personal and Organizational Operating System.

## Release Pipeline

```
Development → Verification → Security Review → Founder Daily Use
→ Closed Alpha → Open Beta → Production → Continuous Improvement
```

No stage may be skipped.

## Founder-First

Every production feature shall first succeed in Founder Daily Use.
The Founder Gap Register (`governance/verification/FOUNDER-GAP-REGISTER.md`)
is the highest-priority product backlog.

## Provider Ecosystem

SHUNYA shall prefer self-hosted, open-source, lifetime-free providers.
Commercial providers are optional, never mandatory. Every provider shall
be replaceable through Provider Adapters. Provider names shall never leak
into business logic.

## Quality Standard

The objective is correctness, elegance, composability, explainability,
reliability, and trust. Every implementation shall improve at least one
of these.

## Success Metric

SHUNYA succeeds when a person or organization can perform their daily work
without needing to leave SHUNYA except when interacting with external
systems or people. Provider integrations shall make external software feel
like native SHUNYA capabilities.
