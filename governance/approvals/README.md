# Approvals

**Path:** `governance/approvals/`

**Purpose:** Record of all approved and rejected engine specifications, phase implementations, and architecture decisions.

---

## Principles

- **Every approval is documented.** Verbal approvals are followed by written confirmation within 24 hours.
- **Approvals are scoped.** An approval authorizes exactly the work described in the associated document — no more, no less.
- **Rejections include reasoning.** A rejection without documented reasoning is not a valid rejection.
- **Approvals may be revoked.** If new information emerges that invalidates the basis of an approval, it may be revoked by the same authority that granted it.
