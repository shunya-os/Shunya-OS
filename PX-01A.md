# PX-01A — Experience Script (Revised)
## The Arrival Journey, from the Visitor's Mind

**Status:** Implementation reference — approved by founder
**Type:** Cinematic narrative, visitor's emotional perspective. UI elements are secondary.
**Guiding principle:** Every visual change must correspond to a change in Reality, Attention, Cognition, or Execution — not to an animation timeline.
**Anti-principle:** If a scene only changes the screen but not the visitor's understanding, redesign it.

---

## 0:00–0:05 — Something is happening

The visitor arrives at `/`.

There is no interface.

There is no headline.

There is no "Loading…"

There is a calm, dark warmth. The visitor does not read anything. They do not need to. The space itself communicates: *Something is here. Something is happening.*

The visitor's first thought is not:

*"What is this website?"*

It is:

*"What is happening?"*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** Nothing yet. The visitor has no model. They are present. They are waiting. The silence is intentional — it creates space for the first signal.

**What SHUNYA is doing:** Nothing. The system is at its first frame. It is not loading. It is waiting.

---

## 0:05–0:08 — The first signal

A single sentence appears. Faint. 24px. Weight 300.

> Too much is happening.

The visitor reads it. It is true. It is universally true. It does not claim to know the visitor's business. It does not claim to know anything about the visitor. It is a statement about the nature of the world.

The visitor might think:

*"Yes."*

*"What does that mean?"*

*"Who said that?"*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor now knows that SHUNYA understands something about the nature of reality. Not the visitor's specific reality — just the nature of it. The visitor has not been sold anything. They have been acknowledged.

**What SHUNYA is doing:** Speaking one sentence. Then waiting.

---

## 0:08–0:18 — Reality begins appearing

From the bottom of the screen, something appears. Not a list. Not a dashboard. Not a card.

A line of text.

> Customer accepted revised proposal.

The visitor reads it. They understand it. They do not need to know what system produced it. They understand the meaning.

Two seconds later, another:

> Supplier delayed shipment by 2 days.

The visitor's mind begins making connections. *A customer and a supplier. These are related, perhaps. Or perhaps they are independent.*

Two seconds later:

> Payment cleared this morning.

The visitor now sees three events. Each is understandable. Each is real. Each could be from any business.

The visitor's thought:

*"Something is happening. I can see it happening."*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor now understands that SHUNYA tracks reality. The visitor has not learned SHUNYA's interface. They have learned SHUNYA's purpose: *knowing what is happening.*

**SHUNYA's Reality Engine is running.** Events are flowing through the SSE Runtime. The Reality Stream is rendering. This is the same Reality Stream that the authenticated product uses.

---

## 0:18–0:25 — One of these matters more

The visitor is reading the three events. None has been highlighted.

Then, without the visitor clicking anything, a subtle shift occurs. The event about the supplier does not change appearance — but a secondary line appears below it, smaller, quieter:

> This affects 3 shipments scheduled this week.

The visitor did not ask for this. SHUNYA offered it. The system determined that this event has consequences.

The other events remain visible. They are not dismissed. But the visitor's attention has been guided.

The visitor's thought:

*"I didn't ask that. It knew that mattered."*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor now understands that SHUNYA does not merely record events. It evaluates them. It knows what matters. The visitor has not been told this. It has been demonstrated.

**SHUNYA's Attention Engine** has scored the events. The supplier delay has a higher priority score because of its downstream consequences. The evidence chain is already prepared.

---

## 0:25–0:35 — Evidence appears

The visitor wonders: *How does it know it affects 3 shipments?*

The visitor clicks on the supplier delay — or touches it, or simply looks at it long enough. The interaction is gentle. No modal. No page transition.

Below the event, an evidence chain expands:

```
Supplier delayed shipment by 2 days.
├── Order #1042 placed July 28
├── Original delivery: August 5
├── Updated delivery: August 7
├── Affected shipments:
│   ├── Shipment #301 (August 5) → moved to August 9
│   ├── Shipment #302 (August 6) → moved to August 10
│   └── Shipment #303 (August 8) → still on track
└── Confidence: 1.0 — data from supplier portal
```

The visitor can see the reasoning. It is not a black box. It is not an AI making claims. It is a chain of evidence.

The visitor's thought:

*"I can see how it knows that."*

*"I can verify this."*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor now understands that SHUNYA is transparent. Every claim is backed by evidence. The visitor does not need to trust SHUNYA — they can verify it.

**SHUNYA's Evidence Runtime** is rendering the evidence chain. The same evidence chain that powers the authenticated product. Nothing is built for the homepage.

---

## 0:35–0:45 — Understanding

The visitor has seen:

- Reality is happening
- SHUNYA knows what matters
- Every claim is backed by evidence

Now, without the visitor clicking anything, a new section appears. Not a panel. Not a modal. It simply becomes visible, as if it was always there.

A quiet observation:

> Supplier delays have occurred 3 times in the last 30 days.

The visitor reads it. They understand it. It is a pattern.

Then, a second observation:

> Two of the three delays were from the same supplier.

The visitor's mind connects: *That supplier is becoming unreliable.*

The visitor has not been told this. They have arrived at it themselves. SHUNYA provided the data. The visitor made the inference.

The visitor's thought:

*"I was about to think that. It said it first."*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor now understands that SHUNYA does not just track events — it recognizes patterns. It sees what the visitor would see, given enough time. SHUNYA saves the visitor time.

**SHUNYA's AI Presence** is producing observations. The same observations that appear in the authenticated Living Workspace.

---

## 0:45–1:00 — Prediction

The visitor has seen: Reality. Attention. Evidence. Understanding.

Now, a new line appears. Not a command. Not a suggestion. A prediction:

> If this supplier delays again this week, 5 shipments will be affected.

The visitor understands the consequence. They did not need to calculate it. SHUNYA calculated it.

The visitor's thought:

*"I should do something about that."*

*"Or I can wait and see what it suggests."*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor now understands that SHUNYA predicts. It does not just record the past — it projects the future. The visitor has not been told "AI-powered predictions." They have seen one.

**SHUNYA's Cognition Engine** is producing the prediction. The data is real. The calculation is real.

---

## 1:00–1:15 — The moment of surprise

The visitor has seen three events:

1. Customer accepted revised proposal.
2. Supplier delayed shipment by 2 days.
3. Payment cleared this morning.

These seem unrelated. A customer, a supplier, a payment. The visitor has not connected them.

Then, quietly, SHUNYA says:

> These are all consequences of the same commitment.

The visitor's mind stops.

*Wait.*

*They are?*

The visitor looks again. The three events are now highlighted. A line connects them — not a drawn line, but an understood relationship. The customer accepted the proposal. That triggered the supplier order. The payment was for the previous phase.

The visitor did not see this connection. The system did.

The visitor's thought:

*"I didn't see that."*

*"It saw something I didn't."*

**This is SHUNYA's defining moment.** The visitor has just experienced something they could not have discovered on their own. The system revealed a hidden relationship. This is not a feature. This is SHUNYA's reason for existing.

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** Everything. The visitor now understands that SHUNYA does not just track events, know what matters, show evidence, recognize patterns, or predict outcomes. It understands causality. It sees the connections between events that the visitor would miss. This is not a faster version of the visitor's own thinking. It is a different kind of thinking.

**SHUNYA's Graph Engine** has traversed the relationships. The Commitment Runtime has linked the events. The relationship was always there — SHUNYA revealed it.

---

## 1:15–1:30 — Reality continues

While the visitor is processing the surprise, another event arrives:

> Task "Review supplier contract" was created.

The visitor did not create this task. SHUNYA created it. The system has determined that the supplier delay warrants a review of the contract.

The visitor's thought:

*"It's still working."*

*"It didn't stop."*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor now understands that SHUNYA is continuous. It does not stop when the visitor stops looking. It does not require the visitor to act. It acts within its own scope.

---

## 1:30–1:40 — The invitation

The visitor has now experienced:

1. Reality is happening (0:05–0:18)
2. SHUNYA knows what matters (0:18–0:25)
3. Every claim is backed by evidence (0:25–0:35)
4. SHUNYA recognizes patterns (0:35–0:45)
5. SHUNYA predicts (0:45–1:00)
6. SHUNYA sees hidden connections (1:00–1:15)
7. SHUNYA is continuous (1:15–1:30)

The visitor has not been sold anything. They have not read any marketing copy. They have experienced SHUNYA.

At the bottom of the screen, a single line appears. It was not there before. It is not a button. It is not a modal. It is a sentence:

> Would you like to make this yours?

The visitor reads it. They understand it. This Reality — the one they have been watching — can become their Reality.

The visitor's thought:

*"Yes."*

*"Not yet."*

*"Let me see more."*

All three are valid. The system waits.

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor understands that what they have been watching is not a demo. It is a shared Reality. They can own it. The invitation is a question, not a demand.

---

## 1:40–1:50 — Authentication

The visitor decides to accept.

The sentence does not change into a button. It does not open a modal. The visitor's cursor is already positioned where the input should be. The email field simply appears — as if it was always waiting for this moment.

The visitor types their email. The screen does not change.

A verification code arrives. The visitor types it. The screen does not change.

The visitor's thought:

*"I am still here. The same place. The same Reality."*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** Authentication did not change the application. It changed the data source. The visitor is in the same workspace. The same Reality Stream is flowing. The same objects are visible. Only now, they are real.

**SHUNYA's Identity Runtime** has authenticated the visitor. The data source has switched from the demonstration tenant to the visitor's tenant. The UI has not changed.

---

## 1:50+ — Reality continues

The visitor is now authenticated.

The same events are visible. The same objects. The same AI observations. The same predictions.

But now:

- The events are the visitor's events
- The objects are the visitor's objects
- The AI is observing the visitor's reality
- The commitments are the visitor's commitments

The visitor's thought:

*"This is mine."*

*"Nothing changed."*

*"It was always mine."*

**What changed in the visitor's mental model:** The visitor now understands that SHUNYA is not an application they logged into. It is a Reality they joined. Authentication was not a gate. It was a personalization event.

The experience does not end. Reality continues flowing. New events arrive. Attention shifts. Cognition updates. Confidence changes. The workspace lives.

## The Defining Moment

The visitor's emotional arc has one peak. It is not the authentication. It is not the AI observations. It is not the prediction.

It is the moment when SHUNYA reveals a connection the visitor did not see.

> These are all consequences of the same commitment.

That single sentence is SHUNYA's value proposition. It is not a feature list. It is not a headline. It is a demonstration of causality that the visitor could not have discovered alone.

After that moment, the visitor knows:

*"SHUNYA sees what I don't."*

Everything after that — trust, invitation, authentication — is downstream of that moment.

---

## Implementation Principles

1. **Every visual change corresponds to a change in Reality, Attention, Cognition, or Execution.** If a visual change does not correspond to one of these, it is decoration. Remove it.

2. **The visitor should never feel that SHUNYA is presenting an interface.** They should feel that SHUNYA is revealing an understanding.

3. **Every scene changes the visitor's mental model.** If a scene only changes the screen but not the visitor's understanding, redesign it.

4. **The experience never resets.** The visitor arrives. Reality is flowing. The visitor joins. Authentication personalizes. Reality continues. The visitor never feels "Now I'm logging in" — they feel "Now this Reality becomes mine."

5. **The invitation is a question, not a CTA.** "Would you like to make this yours?" — no urgency, no button, no modal. The email field simply appears where the cursor is waiting.

6. **The moment of surprise is non-negotiable.** The visitor must experience a connection they did not see. Without this, SHUNYA is a faster version of the visitor's own thinking. With it, SHUNYA is a different kind of thinking.
