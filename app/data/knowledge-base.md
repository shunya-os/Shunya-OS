# Panchi Club Destination Knowledge Base

## Sri Lanka

### Visa
- Indian nationals: ETA required — apply online at eta.gov.lk ($20-$35, 2-3 days)
- Most other nationalities: Free visa on arrival (30 days)
- Passport valid 6+ months required

### Best Time to Visit
- Southwest coast (Galle, Colombo): Dec–Mar (dry)
- East coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay): Apr–Sep (dry)
- Hill country (Kandy, Nuwara Eliya): Jan–Mar (pleasant)

### Currency
- LKR (Sri Lankan Rupee) — 1 INR ≈ 3.5 LKR
- USD widely accepted in tourist areas

### Local Taxes
- VAT: 15% on hotels
- Tourism Development Levy: 1%
- Service charge: 10% (mandatory at most hotels)

### Wedding Requirements (Foreign Nationals)
- Document attestation required (not apostille) — MEA + Sri Lankan High Commission
- Process time: 2-4 weeks
- Affidavit of Single Status from local solicitor
- Original birth certificates
- Valid passports
- Security clearance certificate for some nationalities
- 21-day notice period for Registrar General registration

### Transport
- Train: Colombo–Galle 1st class ~LKR 600-800
- Private driver: $60+/day
- Tuk-tuk: short rides ~LKR 200-500

### Top Venues
- Radisson Blu Resort Galle
- Jetwing Lighthouse, Galle
- Shangri-La Hambantota
- Heritance Kandalama, Dambulla
- Anantara Peace Haven Tangalle

## Bali, Indonesia

### Visa
- Indian nationals: Visa on arrival ($35, 30 days, extendable once)
- Most nationalities: Visa-free or VOA
- Passport valid 6+ months

### Best Time to Visit
- Apr–Oct (dry season, peak)
- Nov–Mar (wet season, fewer crowds)

### Currency
- IDR (Indonesian Rupiah) — 1 INR ≈ 190 IDR
- USD accepted at hotels

### Local Taxes
- Hotel tax: 10%
- Service charge: 5-10%
- Tourist levy: IDR 150,000 (from 2024)

### Wedding Requirements
- Legal marriage requires religious ceremony (Indonesia's marriage law)
- Most foreign couples opt for symbolic ceremony + legal registration at home
- Surat Keterangan (Certificate of No Impediment) from embassy required for legal

### Transport
- Scooter rental: $5-10/day
- Private driver: $35-50/day
- Rideshare (Gojek/Grab): $2-10/ride

### Top Venues
- The Edge Bali (cliff-top)
- Ayana Resort, Jimbaran
- Four Seasons Sayan
- Uluwatu Surf Villas
- Alila Villas Uluwatu

## Maldives

### Visa
- Free visa on arrival for all nationalities (30 days)
- Passport valid 6+ months
- Return ticket + hotel confirmation required

### Best Time to Visit
- Nov–Apr (dry season, peak)
- May–Oct (wet season, lower rates)

### Currency
- MVR (Maldivian Rufiyaa) — USD widely accepted everywhere
- 1 USD ≈ 15 MVR

### Local Taxes
- GST: 12% on accommodation
- Green Tax: $6/person/night
- Service charge: 10%

### Wedding Requirements
- Legal marriage requires 21-day Maldives residency
- Most couples choose symbolic ceremony + legal registration at home
- Certificate of No Impediment from home embassy

### Transport
- Speedboat transfers: $150-600 return (depends on resort distance)
- Seaplane: $300-800 return
- Local ferry: $2-5 (to local islands only)

### Top Venues
- Soneva Fushi
- Anantara Kihavah
- Conrad Maldives Rangali
- COMO Maalifushi
- St. Regis Vommuli

## Thailand

### Visa
- Indian nationals: Visa-free (30 days, from Nov 2024)
- Most nationalities: Visa-free 30-60 days
- Passport valid 6+ months

### Best Time to Visit
- Nov–Feb (cool and dry, best)
- Mar–May (hot)
- Jun–Oct (rainy)

### Currency
- THB (Thai Baht) — 1 INR ≈ 0.43 THB
- 1 USD ≈ 35 THB

### Local Taxes
- VAT: 7%
- Service charge: 10% (common at hotels)

### Wedding Requirements
- Legal marriage straightforward — register at District Office
- Affidavit of Freedom to Marry from home embassy
- Translation of all documents to Thai
- Process: 1-2 weeks document prep + 1 day registration

### Transport
- Tuk-tuk: $2-5
- Grab/Uber: $3-15
- Private driver: $40-70/day
- Domestic flights: $30-80

### Top Venues
- Trisara, Phuket
- Rayavadee, Krabi
- Four Seasons Chiang Mai
- Banyan Tree Bangkok
- Soneva Kiri, Koh Kood

## India (Domestic)

### Popular Destinations
- Goa — beaches, Dec–Feb best
- Kerala — backwaters, Nov–Feb best
- Udaipur — palaces, Oct–Mar best
- Manali/Shimla — mountains, Mar–Jun best
- Andaman Islands — beaches, Nov–Apr best

### Currency
- INR (Indian Rupee)

### Local Taxes
- GST on hotels: 12-18% (under ₹7500: 12%, above: 18%)
- Service charge: optional (mandatory at some luxury hotels)

### Wedding Requirements (Domestic)
- Marriage registration under Special Marriage Act or personal law
- 30-day notice period for Special Marriage Act
- Valid ID proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport)
- Address proof
- Passport-size photos
- Witnesses (3 minimum)

### Transport
- Train: extensive network, vary by class
- Domestic flights: $30-120
- Taxi/rickshaw: varies by city
- Private driver: $20-50/day

### Top Venues
- The Oberoi Amarvilas, Agra
- Taj Lake Palace, Udaipur
- Neemrana Fort Palace
- Coconut Lagoon, Kerala
- Wildflower Hall, Shimla
