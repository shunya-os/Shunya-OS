# Panchi Club Knowledge Base

## Sri Lanka — Destination Weddings

### Visa & Legal Requirements (for Foreigners)
- Valid passport (6+ months validity)
- Original birth certificate
- Unmarried/status affidavit
- Security clearance may be required for certain nationalities
- **Indian couples**: Use MEA attestation + Sri Lankan High Commission legalization (NOT apostille)
- Processing time: 2-4 weeks

### Best Season
- **Southwest coast / Galle area**: November to April (dry season)
- January is excellent for Galle weddings (dry, sunny, low humidity)

### Currency & Costs
- Currency: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR)
- Budget travel: $3-8/day per person
- Mid-range: $15-30/day per person
- Private driver: $60+/day
- Colombo to Galle 1st-class train: ~LKR 600-800

### Tax Considerations
- VAT on hotel/accommodation services
- Tourism development levy applies
- Service charges may apply (typically 10%)

### Venue Knowledge: Radisson Blu Resort Galle
- Location: Galle, Sri Lanka (southwest coast)
- Indian Ocean-facing property
- Beachfront wedding facilities available
- Known for hosting destination weddings
- Accommodates 50-200+ guest weddings
- In-house catering and event planning support

## Best Practices

### Proposals
- Generate multi-format: PDF, infographic, short clip, short movie, text, email body
- Include tax guidance in every proposal
- Offline "executable" itineraries preferred over online links
- Include union/logistics-specific details for ground-level execution

### Lead Management
- Inquiry code format: `PC DD MM YY XX` (space-free, e.g., PC27072601)
- Status pipeline: new → in_progress → converted → cancelled
- Track both guest payments and supplier payments in Tally-like ledger
- Auto-generate invoices with PDF on payment capture
